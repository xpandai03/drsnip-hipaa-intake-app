## 1. Cohort-by-month conversion view: Partial

What exists (only on 335506a, live on v96):

- **Monthly outcomes**, `/admin/outcomes` (`App.tsx:124`, `Outcomes.tsx:319`), API `GET /api/reports/outcomes?metric=outcome_registration|outcome_insurance&from=&to=`.
  - Cohort = the month of the patient's **first** form of that type, in clinic time (`0022:558-567`). Registration and insurance are separate. There is no consultation cohort.
  - Each patient lands in one bucket at the evidence cutoff: Completed / Currently scheduled / Unknown / Neither established.
  - **No rate, no percentage, no completed + scheduled total**, by design (`lib/metrics/outcomes.ts:186-188`, `Outcomes.tsx:13-15`), until the clinic confirms a scope.
- **Patient journeys**, `/admin/journeys`: hard-coded periods (all history, July, August, September, at `Journeys.tsx:100-105`). It only has 7/14/30-day windows (`lib/metrics/registry.ts:10`). The "September 2026" period runs to *today*, and there is no October option.
- **Weekly granularity:** only `/admin/dashboard`, and only for raw submission volume (`api/reports/counts.ts`).

Missing compared with Jeff's ask ("May registrations, how many convert in June, July, August"):

- No share converted as of today.
- No X+1, X+2, X+3 fill.
- No week view.
- `DRSNIP_POST_CALL_REPORTING_BRIEF.md` §6 says the fill is reconstructable **for completions only**. Historical "scheduled" positions need month-end snapshots, and those are not built.

**Counting rule compared with Jeff's:**

- **One count per patient, attributed to the form month:** ✅. The unit is a distinct chart, at the month of its first submission of that form type (`0022:558-567`).
- **"Any appointment on the chart":** ❌. Only appointments that meet all of these count (`0021:173-188`, `0022:595`):
  - not deleted;
  - scheduled on or after the entry day;
  - of one of 3 qualifying profile types (585137, 874151, 585138).
  - Undecided profile types make the patient Unknown.
- **Advance booking:** **fixed.** It counts distinct patients, `bool_or(in_window AND advance)` per patient and then `count(*)` (`0022:234-260`, `api/reports/booking.ts:73,95-97`). It does not count raw appointment records.
